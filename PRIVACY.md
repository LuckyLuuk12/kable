# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 24, 2026**

## Overview

Kable is an unofficial Minecraft launcher designed with privacy and local-first operation in mind.

**Kable does not intentionally collect user data, track user activity, or transmit usage analytics to servers operated by the developer.** Application functionality runs locally on your device, while network communication is used only when necessary to provide features such as authentication, Minecraft integration, and content discovery.

## Data Collection

Kable does not intentionally collect or transmit user data to the developer for analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking purposes.

In particular, Kable does not intentionally operate a telemetry or analytics system that collects:

- Personal information for the developer.
- Application usage statistics or behavioral analytics.
- Performance metrics or crash reports for centralized collection.
- Device fingerprints or location data for tracking purposes.

## Local Data Processing and Storage

Kable processes application data on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

- Application settings and customization preferences.
- Minecraft installation and profile configurations.
- Account identifiers and authentication tokens.
- Locally stored application and Minecraft logs.
- Information about installed mods, resource packs, shader packs, skins, and worlds.

Some of this information may be sensitive. Protect your device and user account accordingly. Data stored locally may be accessible to other software or users with sufficient permissions on your system.

Authentication tokens and other sensitive information are stored according to the application's implementation and the facilities available on your operating system.

## Authentication and External Services

Kable uses external services to provide its functionality. Examples include Microsoft authentication, Minecraft services, version manifests, and mod discovery and download platforms.

When you use these features:

- Authentication is initiated through the application's supported authentication flow.
- Credentials are handled by the relevant authentication provider rather than intentionally collected by the Kable developer.
- Authentication tokens and account information may be processed locally by Kable to authenticate accounts and provide launcher functionality.
- Requests for metadata, mods, resource packs, shader packs, and other content are sent to the relevant external services as needed.

These requests may disclose technical information, such as your IP address, to the services you contact. External providers may also receive account identifiers, authentication information, or request metadata depending on the service and operation.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Kable does not control their data collection or retention practices.

## Data Sharing and Server Infrastructure

Kable does not intentionally send application data to a developer-operated backend for storage, analytics, or user tracking.

The application communicates directly with external services where practical and necessary for its functionality. These connections do not mean that no data leaves your device: information required by an external service is transmitted to that service when you use the corresponding feature.

Kable's public source code is available for inspection. Its privacy practices should be understood in terms of the actual application implementation and the external services it uses.

## Logs and Diagnostics

Kable may generate application and Minecraft logs locally for troubleshooting and debugging.

These logs may contain technical details about application operations, errors, file paths, installed content, or other environment-specific information. Depending on the circumstances, logs may inadvertently contain sensitive information.

Kable does not intentionally upload these logs to the developer for centralized diagnostics or analytics. If you choose to share logs when reporting a problem, review them first and remove authentication tokens, personal information, and other sensitive details.

## Security

Kable aims to minimize unnecessary data transmission and keep application processing local wherever possible. However, no software or storage mechanism can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, protecting locally stored data, and keeping your operating system and application updated.

## Children's Privacy

Kable does not intentionally collect personal information from children or operate a service intended to collect children's data. Users and parents should also consider the privacy practices and age requirements of the external services used with Minecraft.

## Your Privacy Rights

Because Kable does not intentionally collect user data for centralized storage or analytics, the developer generally does not maintain a user profile or analytics record that can be retrieved or deleted on request.

Application data stored locally can be managed through the application, your operating system, and the relevant files or directories. Deleting the application does not necessarily remove every associated file, configuration, or credential.

External services may retain data according to their own policies. Requests concerning that data should be directed to the relevant provider.

Applicable privacy rights depend on your jurisdiction and the circumstances of the processing.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Kable's functionality, external integrations, or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published in this repository, with relevant changes documented where appropriate.

## Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, use [GitHub Issues](../../issues) or [GitHub Discussions](../../discussions). Do not publish authentication tokens, account credentials, or other sensitive information in public reports.

---

Kable is designed to provide a local-first Minecraft launcher experience without developer-operated user tracking or analytics. Network communication remains necessary for certain features and is subject to the privacy practices of the services involved.
